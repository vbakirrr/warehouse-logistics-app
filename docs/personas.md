# Application Personas

## Warehouse Associate

### Goals
- Receive and process warehouse inventory accurately.
- Complete putaway, picking, packing, and replenishment tasks efficiently.

### Daily Tasks
- Receive inventory.
- Put away inventory.
- Pick and pack orders.
- Complete replenishment tasks.
- Report warehouse exceptions.

### Records Created
- Receiving records
- Inventory movement records
- Exception records

### Records Reviewed
- Inventory records
- Picking and packing records
- Task records

### Decisions Made
- Confirm inventory movements.
- Report inventory discrepancies.
- Identify warehouse exceptions.

### Information They Must Not See
- Financial information
- Management-level performance data
- Sensitive employee information

### Frustration / Risk
- Inaccurate inventory information can cause picking delays and operational errors.

### Measurable Success Outcome
- Complete assigned warehouse tasks with at least 98% inventory movement accuracy.

## Inventory Controller

### Goals
- Maintain accurate inventory levels and identify discrepancies quickly.

### Daily Tasks
- Review inventory levels.
- Perform cycle counting.
- Investigate inventory discrepancies.
- Monitor replenishment needs.

### Records Created
- Cycle count records
- Inventory adjustment records
- Discrepancy records

### Records Reviewed
- Inventory records
- Cycle count records
- Inventory movement records

### Decisions Made
- Determine when inventory requires adjustment.
- Identify items requiring replenishment.
- Escalate significant discrepancies.

### Information They Must Not See
- Financial information
- Sensitive employee information
- Management-only operational decisions

### Frustration / Risk
- Inventory discrepancies can reduce inventory accuracy and disrupt warehouse operations.

### Measurable Success Outcome
- Maintain inventory accuracy at or above 98%.

## Logistics Coordinator

### Goals
- Coordinate picking, packing, and shipping activities accurately and on time.

### Daily Tasks
- Review orders ready for shipment.
- Coordinate picking and packing.
- Monitor shipping activities.
- Handle shipping exceptions.

### Records Created
- Shipping records
- Shipment exception records

### Records Reviewed
- Order records
- Picking records
- Packing records
- Shipping records

### Decisions Made
- Prioritize shipping exceptions.
- Coordinate shipment resolution.
- Determine when an exception requires escalation.

### Information They Must Not See
- Financial information
- Sensitive employee information
- Management-only performance information

### Frustration / Risk
- Delayed or inaccurate shipping information can cause missed deliveries.

### Measurable Success Outcome
- Process at least 95% of assigned shipments within the expected operational timeframe.

## Operations Manager

### Goals
- Monitor warehouse performance and make operational decisions.

### Daily Tasks
- Review warehouse activity.
- Monitor inventory and fulfillment performance.
- Review exceptions.
- Identify operational improvement opportunities.

### Records Created
- Management decisions
- Operational exception records

### Records Reviewed
- Inventory records
- Warehouse activity records
- Shipping records
- Exception records

### Decisions Made
- Prioritize operational issues.
- Approve corrective actions.
- Decide when issues require escalation.

### Information They Must Not See
- Passwords and access credentials
- Sensitive personal information not required for operations

### Frustration / Risk
- Limited visibility into warehouse operations can delay corrective decisions.

### Measurable Success Outcome
- Reduce unresolved operational exceptions by 20%.
